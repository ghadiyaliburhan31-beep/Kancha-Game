KANCHA USTAAD GUIDE

Controls, features, smart bulb bank shots & rules

👆 1. Drag Backward to Aim (Slingshot Control)

Touch or click on your Striker marble (the goti with the white dashed circle) and pull backwards in the opposite direction of where you want to shoot.

📏 2. Power & Trajectory Dots

Pulling farther back increases flick power. The white dotted trajectory line shows the initial release line and angle. Release your finger to shoot!

📍 3. "Jahan Ruki Wahin Se" (Street Rule)

Your striker does not teleport back to base! You always take your next shot from wherever the marble came to a complete stop on the dirt pitch.

⭕ 4. True Circle Knockout Win Condition

To clear a level, you must knock target marbles 100% completely outside the chalk ring before running out of shots.

💡 Permanent Aiming Arrow (No Timer!)

Tap the 💡 Bulb button in the HUD. A bright golden guide line locks onto the best target goti. It never disappears until you actually flick the marble, so you can plan your shot calmly.

⚡ Smart Cross Hit (Bank Shot Around Walls)

When a red brick or obstacle blocks the direct path, the bulb automatically computes a ricochet bank shot off the side cushions! Follow the two-legged golden path bouncing off the cushion around the wall into the target.

🪙 How to Get More Bulbs

You receive a free daily hint bulb every day upon opening the game! You can also buy bulbs in the store for 20 Coins earned by clearing levels.

🧱 Broken Red Bricks (Laal Eent)

Hard terracotta obstacles placed at tricky angles. Your marble rebounds off them with 82% bounce retention. Use them for trick bank shots!

🪨 River Pebbles (Kankad)

Heavy rounded stones scattered across the street. Direct impacts deflect your marble, so aim carefully around them.

🌧️ Rainy Water Puddles (Hydro-Drag)

Glossy water puddles with mud. Rolling through puddles creates splash particles and slows your goti down unless you equip a high-grip striker.

🕳️ Gucchu (The Dirt Pit)

In Tier 3 levels, a traditional dirt hole is dug in the center. Sinking target marbles or your striker into the Gucchu earns maximum stars!

☠️ Black Penalty Gotis

Do NOT touch or hit black gotis! Knocking a penalty marble out costs you penalty shots and drops your star score.

🏋️ Mass / Power

Determines kinetic momentum transfer. Heavier strikers (like Sunehra Raja and Maharaja Gold) hit like a cannonball to blast clusters of gotis out in one hit.

💧 Puddle Grip

Strikers with high grip (like Billi Ki Aankh / Cat's Eye and Neelam) glide right through rainwater puddles with minimal speed loss.

🏓 Rebound / Bounce

Higher rebound stat preserves velocity when bouncing off walls, red bricks, and cushions for long-range trick shots.

🪙 Galli vs 💎 Pro Strikers

Unlock Galli strikers using coins earned from winning levels. Pro strikers feature tournament-grade diamond and gold specs.

🗺️ Snakes & Ladders 50-Level Trail

50 uniquely designed levels across 5 mohalla tiers. Climb ladders to skip ahead, but watch out for tricky snakes!

⏩ Forward Unlock (+2 Levels)

Every time you beat a level, the game automatically unlocks the next 2 levels ahead so you always have choices in your journey.

🤖 Chintu (AI Bot Mode)

Play a turn-based street duel against the neighborhood AI champion. First player to eliminate 4 gotis wins the match.

👥 Pass-and-Play (2-Player Mode)

Compete with a friend on the same phone or tablet screen, taking turns with green and red strikers.
